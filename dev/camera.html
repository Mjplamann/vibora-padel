Fallback controls

Move the mouse over the player view: the racket follows the pointer on a plane 0.65 m ahead of the eye. Flick through the ball to hit. WASD / arrows move. The strobe shows the auto-swing every 1/60 s; ● ball, ● contact. Orange face = forehand face, dark = back face.
